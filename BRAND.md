# CoreX Signal AI Brand

## Official identity

**Brand:** CoreX Signal AI  
**Official website:** https://corexsignalai.com/  
**Official GitHub organization:** https://github.com/corexsignalai  
**Official contact:** info@corexsignalai.com

CoreX Signal AI is positioned as an **AI-powered quantitative market intelligence and signal analytics platform**.

## Core messaging pillars

Public brand communication should emphasize:

- AI-powered quantitative market intelligence;
- explainable signal analytics;
- multi-timeframe coherence;
- multi-market monitoring;
- strategy selection and risk profiles;
- 11-language access;
- dynamic risk context;
- historical setup / Win-Rate analytics;
- probabilistic forecasting;
- macro, regime, depth and factor context.

## Accuracy rules

Public material must not:

- guarantee returns or trading success;
- present historical Win Rate as guaranteed future performance;
- claim that CoreX Signal AI directly executes trades;
- publish proprietary algorithms, credentials or internal security configuration;
- imply endorsement by third-party brands merely because a technology is referenced or integrated;
- present simulated or illustrative metrics as verified live performance.

## Visual direction

The CoreX Signal AI visual language is:

**dark quantitative terminal · deep navy / black · cyan / emerald intelligence accents · clear hierarchy · restrained glow · readable data density**

Marketing assets should feel technical and premium without sacrificing legibility.

## Screenshots

Dashboard screenshots may be used to demonstrate the product experience. Point-in-time metrics in screenshots should be treated as dynamic examples and not as guaranteed results.

## Trademark / usage

The CoreX Signal AI name, logo and product imagery should not be used in a way that implies ownership, partnership, certification or endorsement without authorization.
