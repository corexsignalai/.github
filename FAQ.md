# CoreX Signal AI — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is CoreX Signal AI?

CoreX Signal AI is an **AI-powered quantitative market intelligence and signal analytics platform**. It combines real-time market context, explainable signal reasoning, multi-timeframe coherence, strategy profiles, dynamic risk context, probabilistic forecasting, institutional depth mapping, macro intelligence and historical setup analytics in one workspace.

## Does CoreX Signal AI execute trades automatically?

No. CoreX Signal AI is designed for **market intelligence and decision support**. It does not directly execute trades.

## What decision states can the dashboard show?

The public dashboard can present **BUY, SELL, HOLD and VETO / Active Protection** states.

## What is Active Protection?

Active Protection is a visible no-entry state used when an inhibitor or risk condition makes entry unsuitable. The dashboard can show the reason, recommended action, profile-adjusted context and automatic re-scanning while conditions are re-evaluated.

## Which timeframes are supported in the dashboard?

The public dashboard uses a nine-timeframe hierarchy:

`M1 · M5 · M15 · M30 · H1 · H4 · D1 · W1 · MN1`

## Which markets are shown in the current dashboard?

Current public dashboard examples include:

`EURUSD` · `GBPUSD` · `USDJPY` · `USDCAD` · `XAUUSD` · `Bitcoin` · `Ethereum`

The broader product presentation also references multi-market intelligence across Forex, Crypto, Gold and selected equity / stock contexts.

## What strategy profiles are available?

CoreX Signal AI publicly presents four strategy / risk profiles:

- Conservative `×0.50`
- Balanced `×1.00`
- Aggressive `×1.50`
- Scalper `×2.00`

## Does the platform explain why a signal exists?

Yes. The public product is designed around **explainable AI**. Visible reasoning can include factors such as trend strength, PCA, momentum, slope gradient, Hurst, flow imbalance, Dynamic Time-Warp matching, volatility / regime effects and divergence context.

## What is Cross-Timeframe Coherence?

Cross-Timeframe Coherence shows whether multiple timeframes agree or conflict. The dashboard keeps disagreement visible instead of collapsing all timeframes into one directional label.

## What is Execution Optimization?

Execution Optimization is an analytical planning layer that can show context such as `R:R`, lot size, Stop Loss, Entry, Take Profit, risk budget, ATR and distance metrics. It does not place orders.

## What is the Probabilistic Forecast Engine?

It presents scenario distributions and confidence ranges instead of claiming one guaranteed future price. The public interface can display a 95% confidence interval, multiple simulated paths and lower / median / upper scenarios.

## What is the Institutional Depth Map?

The Institutional Depth Map adds structural context around the current price using supply / demand zones and FVG / OB context.

## Does CoreX Signal AI show historical performance analytics?

The dashboard can show historical or simulated analytical metrics such as Return, Maximum Drawdown, Win Rate, Signal Count and an equity curve. These figures are contextual analytics and do not guarantee future performance.

## Does the product include macro or news context?

Yes. The Macro Intelligence Feed can include headlines, sentiment context and upcoming market-session windows.

## How many languages does the product support?

The public product experience presents an **11-language interface**.

## Who developed the CoreX Signal AI infrastructure?

CoreX Signal AI is built on **proprietary technology infrastructure designed and developed by CoreX Capital AI**.

Third-party technologies referenced in public documentation support functions such as connectivity, market data, visualization, storage, security, infrastructure or delivery. They are not the developer or owner of the CoreX Signal AI core technology.

## Is CoreX Signal AI a broker?

CoreX Signal AI is presented as a market-intelligence and signal-analytics product. It is not described as the entity executing user trades.
