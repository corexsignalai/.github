# CoreX Signal AI — Official Product Overview

**CoreX Signal AI** is an AI-powered quantitative market intelligence and signal analytics platform designed to turn complex multi-market information into a clear, high-speed decision workspace.

The product experience combines **real-time market context, explainable signal reasoning, multi-timeframe coherence, strategy profiles, dynamic risk context, probabilistic forecasting, institutional depth mapping, macro intelligence and historical setup analytics** in one interface.

## Technology origin

**CoreX Signal AI is built on proprietary technology infrastructure designed and developed by CoreX Capital AI.**

CoreX Capital AI is the developer of the product's core technology infrastructure. Third-party platforms and providers referenced in the CoreX Signal AI ecosystem support functions such as integration, connectivity, market data, visualization, storage, infrastructure and delivery; they are not the developer or owner of the CoreX Signal AI core technology.

## Core product proposition

**One dashboard. Multiple intelligence layers.**

CoreX Signal AI is designed to help users answer four questions quickly:

1. **What is the current market / signal state?**
2. **How much agreement exists across timeframes and factors?**
3. **What risk, regime and macro context surrounds the decision?**
4. **What historical and probabilistic evidence supports or challenges it?**

## Explainability

The public product is intentionally designed around **human-readable AI** rather than a black-box signal badge. Visible reasoning can include factors such as PCA, trend strength, momentum, gradient, Hurst, flow imbalance, DTW, volatility / regime effects and divergence context.

## Product scope

CoreX Signal AI is a **market intelligence and decision-support system**. It does not directly execute trades.

Historical, simulated or illustrative metrics — including Win Rate, Expected R, return, drawdown, confidence and signal count — are analytical context and do not guarantee future performance.
