# CoreX Signal AI — Forex, Gold & Crypto Market Intelligence

CoreX Signal AI applies the same explainable quantitative intelligence workflow across multiple market contexts, helping users compare instruments without changing analytical methodology.

## Current dashboard symbol examples

The public dashboard currently highlights examples including:

`EURUSD` · `GBPUSD` · `USDJPY` · `USDCAD` · `XAUUSD` · `Bitcoin` · `Ethereum`

The broader product presentation also references multi-market intelligence across **Forex, Gold, Crypto and selected equity / stock contexts**.

## Forex intelligence

Forex analysis can combine:

- directional bias;
- nine-timeframe coherence;
- trend / momentum factor context;
- risk and readiness state;
- probabilistic forecast scenarios;
- session context;
- macro headline awareness;
- secondary timeframe confirmation or conflict.

The goal is to show not only a directional idea, but also whether short-term and higher-timeframe structures agree.

## XAUUSD / Gold intelligence

Gold can behave differently from major FX pairs because macro events, risk sentiment, volatility and session transitions can have a stronger impact on price behavior.

CoreX Signal AI places XAUUSD inside the same broader intelligence stack:

- AI Decision Output;
- Cross-Timeframe Coherence;
- Strategic Console;
- Execution Optimization;
- Probabilistic Forecast Engine;
- Institutional Depth Map;
- Market Regime Pulse;
- Macro Intelligence Feed;
- Model Interpretation;
- Factor Intelligence.

This makes the dashboard useful for understanding **direction, uncertainty, structure and risk together** rather than relying on one indicator.

## Crypto intelligence

Bitcoin and Ethereum can be evaluated using the same explainable decision framework, while preserving their own volatility and regime context.

The dashboard can surface:

- BUY / SELL / HOLD / VETO state;
- short-term vs higher-timeframe agreement;
- confidence and readiness;
- volatility / regime effects;
- forecast distribution;
- factor attribution;
- profile-aware risk context.

## Nine-timeframe analysis

The common timeframe hierarchy is:

`M1 · M5 · M15 · M30 · H1 · H4 · D1 · W1 · MN1`

This lets users inspect tactical and structural context within the same workflow.

## Strategy profiles

The public dashboard exposes four strategy / risk profiles:

| Profile | Multiplier | Intended behavior |
|---|---:|---|
| Conservative | `×0.50` | stricter filtering and highest-conviction selection |
| Balanced | `×1.00` | balanced protection and opportunity frequency |
| Aggressive | `×1.50` | more opportunities with softer veto behavior |
| Scalper | `×2.00` | maximum short-term tactical frequency |
