# CoreX Signal AI — Product Use Cases

CoreX Signal AI is designed for users and teams that need **explainable, multi-layered market intelligence** rather than a single opaque signal badge.

## 1. Multi-timeframe market review

A user can compare `M1 · M5 · M15 · M30 · H1 · H4 · D1 · W1 · MN1` within one workflow to understand whether tactical price action agrees with broader market structure.

Useful for:

- identifying agreement or conflict across timeframes;
- separating short-term noise from higher-timeframe structure;
- understanding why a market remains HOLD even when one timeframe is directional.

## 2. AI signal interpretation

The AI Decision Output combines the action state with readable context. Depending on conditions, the system can display **BUY, SELL, HOLD or VETO / Active Protection**.

Useful for:

- understanding the current recommended state;
- reviewing confidence, alignment and readiness;
- inspecting the factors or inhibitors behind the decision;
- seeing why the system is acting or waiting.

## 3. Risk-aware trade planning

The Execution Optimization module translates the analytical state into planning context such as:

`R:R · Lot Size · Stop Loss · Entry · Take Profit · Risk Budget · ATR · TP Distance · SL Distance`

This supports risk-aware planning without turning the product into an automatic execution terminal.

## 4. Active Protection and no-trade decisions

A high-quality decision system should be able to recommend **not entering**.

The VETO / Active Protection workflow can surface:

- the protection state;
- inhibitor / meta-analysis context;
- recommended action;
- automatic re-scanning;
- different impact across Scalp, Day Trade and Swing horizons.

This is useful when market conditions are conflicted, weak or unsuitable for entry.

## 5. Scenario-based forecasting

The Probabilistic Forecast Engine presents a range of plausible outcomes through confidence intervals and scenario distributions rather than one guaranteed target.

Useful for:

- visualizing uncertainty;
- comparing lower / median / upper scenarios;
- understanding directional spread and forecast range.

## 6. Market-structure review

The Institutional Depth Map adds structural context around spot price through supply / demand zones and FVG / OB context.

Useful for:

- reviewing structural zones around the current price;
- combining direction with market structure;
- comparing the active signal with nearby institutional context.

## 7. Explainable model review

The Model Interpretation Layer and Factor Intelligence Hub expose readable factor context and ranked impact drivers.

Useful for:

- understanding which factors support or oppose a signal;
- distinguishing momentum, trend, volatility and regime effects;
- reviewing factor disagreement rather than hiding it.

## 8. Macro and session awareness

The Macro Intelligence Feed keeps headline, sentiment and session-window context inside the same workspace.

Useful for:

- checking whether a model signal is occurring around important macro information;
- seeing upcoming market-session transitions;
- keeping technical and macro context together.

## 9. Multi-market monitoring

Current dashboard examples include:

`EURUSD` · `GBPUSD` · `USDJPY` · `USDCAD` · `XAUUSD` · `Bitcoin` · `Ethereum`

Users can move between supported instruments while keeping the same analytical framework.

## 10. Strategy-profile personalization

CoreX Signal AI publicly exposes four strategy / risk profiles:

- **Conservative** — stricter filtering;
- **Balanced** — balanced protection and opportunity frequency;
- **Aggressive** — more opportunities with softer veto behavior;
- **Scalper** — maximum short-term tactical frequency.

This allows the same market to be interpreted in a way that better reflects the selected strategy style.

## Who can benefit from the platform?

The product is positioned for:

- individual traders seeking a structured analytical workspace;
- trading teams that need a common market-intelligence view;
- fintech businesses and white-label operators evaluating an explainable signal layer;
- users who want multi-market, multi-timeframe context in one dashboard.
