# Code of Conduct

CoreX Signal AI maintains this public GitHub space for professional product documentation, brand information and community interaction.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- keep discussion relevant to public documentation and product information;
- protect privacy and confidential information;
- avoid spam, harassment, impersonation and deceptive claims;
- avoid posting credentials, personal data or security-sensitive material;
- avoid publishing unsupported claims about performance, regulation, partnerships or endorsements.

Content that creates security, privacy, legal or abuse risk may be removed.

For product support, use the official channels in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
