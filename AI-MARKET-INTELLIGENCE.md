# CoreX Signal AI — AI Market Intelligence

CoreX Signal AI is an **AI-powered quantitative market intelligence and signal analytics platform** designed to convert complex, multi-market information into an explainable decision-support workspace.

The product brings together market state, signal reasoning, multi-timeframe structure, risk context, probabilistic scenarios, regime analysis, macro awareness and historical setup analytics in one interface.

## What AI market intelligence means in CoreX Signal AI

CoreX Signal AI does not treat a market signal as an isolated BUY or SELL label. A decision is evaluated in context:

- **directional state** — BUY, SELL, HOLD or VETO / Active Protection;
- **cross-timeframe coherence** — agreement and conflict from `M1` through `MN1`;
- **model confidence and composite context**;
- **risk and readiness state**;
- **strategy / risk profile** — Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive or Scalper;
- **execution-planning context** — R:R, ATR, SL / Entry / TP geometry and risk budget;
- **probabilistic forecast distribution**;
- **institutional supply / demand context**;
- **model interpretation and factor attribution**;
- **market regime and structural behavior**;
- **macro / news awareness**;
- **historical or simulated setup statistics**.

## Explainable decision support

The platform is built around **human-readable AI**. Visible reasoning may include contributors such as trend strength, PCA principal factors, momentum differential, slope gradient, Hurst trend exponent, flow imbalance, Dynamic Time-Warp matching, volatility / regime effects, divergence context and directional inhibitors.

The user-facing chain is designed to remain understandable:

> **Market state → timeframe agreement → AI decision → risk / protection → scenario range → structural context → readable reasoning**

## Multi-market intelligence

Current dashboard examples include:

`EURUSD` · `GBPUSD` · `USDJPY` · `USDCAD` · `XAUUSD` · `Bitcoin` · `Ethereum`

The same analytical framework is used across supported market contexts so users can compare different instruments without learning a different workflow for each one.

## Multi-timeframe intelligence

The dashboard uses a nine-timeframe hierarchy:

`M1 · M5 · M15 · M30 · H1 · H4 · D1 · W1 · MN1`

This makes short-term flow and higher-timeframe structure visible at the same time. Disagreement is not hidden; a market can show a SELL state on one timeframe while other timeframes remain HOLD.

## Active Protection

A core part of CoreX Signal AI is the ability to recommend **not entering** when conditions are unsuitable. A VETO / Active Protection state can expose the inhibitor, recommended action, profile-adjusted context and automatic re-scanning while conditions are re-evaluated.

## Designed and developed by CoreX Capital AI

CoreX Signal AI is built on **proprietary technology infrastructure designed and developed by CoreX Capital AI**. Third-party services referenced in public documentation are used for functions such as connectivity, data, visualization, storage, delivery or infrastructure support; they are not the developer or owner of the CoreX Signal AI core technology.

## Product scope

CoreX Signal AI provides **market intelligence and decision support**. It does not directly execute trades. Historical, simulated or illustrative figures — including Win Rate, Expected R, return, drawdown, confidence and signal count — are analytical context and do not guarantee future performance.
